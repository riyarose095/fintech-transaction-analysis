# Fintech Transaction Analysis  

End-to-end fintech transaction analysis using Databricks SQL + Power BI, delivering insights on customer segmentation, payment preferences, and repayment risk to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔹 Project Workflow  
1. **Data Preparation** – Cleaned and transformed data using Databricks SQL.  
2. **Data Visualization** – Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs and charts.  
3. **Insights & Reporting** – Highlighted key metrics such as revenue trends, customer activity, and default rates.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- 💰 Processed ₹23.2M in transactions across 2 years, highlighting revenue growth trends.
- 👥 Analyzed ~1,000 unique customers, segmented by age group, gender, and credit score.
- 💳 Found Credit Card (45%) and Wallet (30%) as the dominant payment methods.
- 📈 Identified 40% YoY growth in digital wallet usage, showing adoption of cashless fintech solutions.
- ⚠️ Customers with credit score <600 had 3x higher default rates, signaling risk factors.

## 📂 Dataset  
The dataset used is a synthetic fintech transaction dataset containing ~5,000 records with attributes such as:  
- Customer demographics (Age, Gender, Credit Score)  
- Transaction details (Amount, Date, Payment Method)  
- Repayment & default status  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **SQL (Databricks)** – Data cleaning, joins, and aggregations  
- **Power BI** – Dashboard design & visualization  
- **GitHub** – Project hosting & documentation

---

## 📊 Insights & Key Analysis  

- **Customer Segmentation**: Grouped customers by transaction frequency, spend, and repayment behavior to identify profitable and risky segments.  
- **High vs. Low Value Customers**: Top 20% customers generated ~**60%** of revenue.  
- **Risk Analysis**: Young customers (**18–24**) with low credit scores showed higher default rates.  
- **Spending Trends**: E-commerce and Travel were top categories; Healthcare, though fewer in count, had higher average spend.  

---

## 💡 Business Impact & Recommendations  
- Enable targeted marketing by focusing on high-value customer segments (top 20%).  
- Promote wallet adoption through cashback offers, as adoption is already growing 40% YoY.  
- Strengthen credit risk checks for customers with scores <600 to reduce default rates.  
- Partner with travel & e-commerce merchants for cross-sell opportunities since they drive the most spend.
